# More booking sites

Google Hotels and Trivago list most booking sites between them. A few more are worth a look in some countries. Check them only when the user books from that country and the site isn't already in one of the two lists. Each takes a minute or two.

Items marked *Checked* were tested live in Oct 2026, from Australia. The rest are leads that haven't been tested; if one doesn't work as described, say so and move on.

## Regional booking sites
- **Australia:** Wotif, Webjet, Luxury Escapes and Qantas Hotels. Luxury Escapes and Qantas Hotels appeared in Google's list in testing (*Checked*). Qantas Hotels is often a little dearer.
- **UK:** lastminute.com, and Secret Escapes (free membership).
- **Elsewhere:** HRS (Germany), Rakuten Travel and Jalan (Japan).

## Last-minute
- **HotelTonight:** only for stays starting within about a week. It mostly works through its app.

## What to skip
- **Hidden-hotel deals** (Hotwire Hot Rates, Priceline Express Deals): they hide the hotel's name until you pay, so they can't confirm the hotel the user asked about.
- **Flight + hotel packages:** a different product. Mention them only if the user is also booking flights.
