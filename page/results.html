    Questions

    Everything you might want to know

    We don't rank anything.

    The cheapest confirmed price always comes first, whether or not a site pays us. We want you to get the cheapest price.

    What is MyRoomPrice?

    A free tool for Claude that finds the cheapest way to book a hotel you've already chosen. Tell it the hotel and your dates, and Claude finds that hotel on the big booking sites and its own website, reads each price live, then shows them side by side, with taxes and fees included.

    Why use it instead of a comparison site?

    Comparison sites show listed prices, which are often per night, before tax, or out of date, and they rarely show the hotel's own member rates. MyRoomPrice opens each site live in your browser, reads the real total for your whole stay, and puts the hotel's own website alongside.

    It also tells you what you give up for a lower price, such as free cancellation, and finds recent reviews of the hotel while it checks.

    Do you rank results for money?

    No. We don't rank anything. Results are always sorted by the total you'd pay, cheapest first, and you can filter and re-sort them yourself. We want you to get the cheapest price.

    Some booking links go through our partner Stay22 and may earn us a small commission, at no extra cost to you. Whether a site pays us never changes where it appears: the cheapest confirmed price comes first, even when it pays us nothing.

    Why does Chrome say “Claude started debugging this browser”?

    That's normal for MyRoomPrice. Chrome shows this bar whenever an extension, here Claude in Chrome, is working in your browser, and it goes away when Claude finishes.

    While a check is running, don't click Cancel on that bar and don't close the background tab Claude is using: either one stops the check. Claude uses just one background tab, besides this page, and closes it when it's done.

    Is it free?

    Yes, MyRoomPrice is free. It runs inside Claude, so you need a Claude plan that includes Claude in Chrome.

    What happens to my data?

    Your search stays in your browser. Results travel inside this page's own link, after the #, which browsers never send to a server, so we never see them. Claude visits the booking sites in your own browser, just as you would. A review video loads from YouTube only when you choose to play it. Your recent searches are kept only in this browser, so you can check one again in a click; clear them any time with Clear all.

    Can I rely on the prices?

    They're the prices each site showed when Claude checked. Prices and availability change often, some sites add fees at checkout, and we can't guarantee any price. Always check the total and the cancellation terms on the booking site before you pay.
